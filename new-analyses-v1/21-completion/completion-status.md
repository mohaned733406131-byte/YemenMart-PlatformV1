## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** YemenMart  
**Project Type:** Multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Market:** Yemen (MENA region expansion)  
**Go-Live Date:** 2026-12-13

### 1.1 Project Summary

YemenMart is a 100% custom-built multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace designed specifically for the Yemeni market. The platform addresses unique local challenges including limited banking infrastructure, mobile-first internet usage, Arabic-first communication, and complex regional logistics across 17 governates.
